About DailyClaimIt

DailyClaimIt is a small, independent team that curates free offers: sweepstakes, paid surveys and quizzes, rewards programs, and free stuff. Our goal is simple: make it easier to find legitimate opportunities, and make it just as easy to understand the fine print before you sign up.

What we do

We review offers available through affiliate networks and list the ones that look legitimate and clearly explain what you get and what's required. We sort them into Sweepstakes, Surveys & Quizzes, and Free Stuff, and we update listings as offers change or end. We also publish guides on how these programs work, what to realistically expect, and how to avoid scams.

How we vet offers

  • Free to start. We don't list offers that require payment just to enter a sweepstakes or claim a prize.
  • Clear terms. The offer page should explain eligibility, requirements, and, for sweepstakes, official rules with an identifiable sponsor.
  • Reputable sources. We work with established affiliate networks and advertisers.
  • Ongoing checks. We review listings regularly and remove offers that expire, stop working, or no longer meet our standards.

We can't guarantee any offer, prize, or reward. Each offer is run by the advertiser, under their own terms. If you spot a problem with a listing, please tell us.

How we make money

DailyClaimIt is free to use. We earn affiliate commissions when users complete some offers listed on the site, for example signing up for a rewards program or entering a sweepstakes. This doesn't cost you anything extra. Read our full affiliate disclosure.
