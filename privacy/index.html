Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy explains what information DailyClaimIt ("we", "us") collects on dailyclaimit.com and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Email address — only if you subscribe to our email list. It is sent to and stored in a Google Sheet via Google Apps Script, with the time and the page you signed up from.
  • Usage data — we use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the site. Clarity collects device and browser information, pages viewed, clicks, scrolls and session recordings, and uses cookies or similar technology. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • Hosting and security data — our hosting and CDN providers (GitHub Pages and Cloudflare) may process IP addresses and request logs to deliver and protect the site.

Affiliate partners

Offers on this site are affiliate links through networks such as MaxBounty and Glitchy. When you click an offer you leave DailyClaimIt; the network and advertiser may set cookies and record your click (including a tracking ID that tells them the click came from us). Any information you submit on an advertiser's page is governed by their privacy policy, not ours. We do not receive the personal information you give to advertisers; we may receive aggregated reports of clicks and completed offers.

How we use information

  • To send the emails you signed up for, which may include sponsored offers.
  • To understand and improve the site.
  • To track affiliate performance.

Sharing

We never sell or share your email address. It is stored only with the service provider we use to run our email list (Google) and is not given to advertisers or affiliate partners. Affiliate partners and advertisers receive personal information only if you submit it yourself on their offer page. We may disclose information where required by law.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in any email or by writing to us. You can ask us to delete your email address by contacting contact@dailyclaimit.com. You can block cookies in your browser settings. Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK), you may have additional rights to access, correct or delete your data.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 (and many offers require users to be 18+). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
